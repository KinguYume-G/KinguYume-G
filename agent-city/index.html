How the city thinks

Every resident evaluates competing needs each simulated hour: work, food, rest, health and social life. Local wages, taxes, employment and market prices influence their decisions. The simulation uses utility-based autonomous agents, not an external LLM.

All computation runs in your browser. Each visitor has a separate city; save data is local to the browser.

Welcome to Atlas

You are the mayor of a living city. Residents make independent decisions based on hunger, energy, money, happiness and health. Your decisions change their world.

Build homes and jobs, set taxes and services, inspect citizens, and run experiments. Use the speed controls to advance time, or pause to plan. Reach 120 people and 70 happiness by day 30.

No sign-in or API key required. Save stores your city on this device only.
